Our Services

We Fix the Appliance That's Giving You Trouble

We work on all major brands and show up with the most common parts already on the truck, so we can get it done in a single visit whenever possible.

Refrigerators

Not cooling, leaking water or making a strange noise? We check the compressor, thermostat and defrost system.

Washers

Leaks, a drum that won't spin, water that won't drain or too much shaking. We get it washing right again.

Dryers

No heat, two cycles to dry one load or shutting off on its own. We clean out the vent line too.

Dishwashers

Dishes coming out dirty, standing water or leaks underneath. We check the pump, spray arms and seals.

Ovens & Ranges

Burners that won't light, an oven that never hits temperature or controls that don't respond.

Microwaves

Not heating, sparking or a turntable that won't spin. Safe diagnosis of the magnetron, door and panel.

Freezers

Frost building up, temperature climbing or a motor that won't start. We save your food in time.
